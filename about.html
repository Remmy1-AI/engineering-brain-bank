About Engineering Brain Bank

KB4GESA / Korle Boye · Learn • Share • Succeed

Engineering Brain Bank is a one-stop platform for engineering students to quickly find, access, and share academic materials — course slides, lecture notes, past questions, and peer-shared resources — under the KB4GESA / Korle Boye initiative.

It is more than a PDF repository. It represents student collaboration, academic support, knowledge sharing, and accessibility for Korle Boye engineers.

How materials are organized

Department→ Level→ Semester→ Course→ Topic→ Material

Example: Civil Engineering → Level 400 → Semester 1 → CE 401 → Structural Analysis → Lecture Slides.

FAQs

Who can upload?

Any engineering student with useful materials. Uploads go through a review before publishing.

Is this an official faculty site?

No — it is a student-led knowledge bank connected to KB4GESA / Korle Boye.

How do I search?

Use “Ask the Brain Bank” on the home page, or filters on Courses and Resources.
